**Description:**

Roster Board is the shift calendar built by a shift worker, for shift workers.

YOUR ROSTER, AT A GLANCE
- Colour-coded calendar with your own shift types: days, nights, training, anything
- Start and finish times work out your hours automatically
- Build your rotation once, then fill your calendar for a year in one tap

EXCESS HOURS AND OVERTIME
- Log excess hours and overtime on the same shift. Excess first, then overtime,
  the way it's actually worked
- See totals for excess, overtime and ordinary hours every month and year

KNOW YOUR PAY
- Enter your hourly rate once
- Set your own excess and overtime rates
- Get an estimated pay breakdown for any month or year

LEAVE, SWAPS AND LIFE
- Track annual and sick leave on the same calendar
- Record shift swaps with who and why
- Add appointments, reminders and birthdays alongside your shifts

REPORTS
- Hours by shift type, weekday vs weekend and comparison with last month
- A year in review: total hours, busiest month, overtime and leave
- Export a polished PDF of your monthly roster

SAFE AND SHARED
- Sign in to back up automatically and use Roster Board on all your devices
- Recover from earlier backups if something goes missing
- Share your work roster with a partner or colleague. Your personal events and
  pay are never shared
